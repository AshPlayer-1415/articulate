# Evaluation limits

Articulate offers reviewed collaboration preferences. Automated tests check
specific invariants such as scope, consent, revisions, explicit overrides and
known injection attempts. They cannot certify all generated answers as correct.
Preference or satisfaction ratings do not establish factual accuracy, learning
gains, comprehension, personality or intelligence.

For comparisons, freeze tasks, candidate, reference results and rubric before
generation. Preserve first attempts, provider errors and incomplete runs. Keep
format checks, factual review and human preference ratings separate. After a case
informs a change, use it as a regression rather than a fresh holdout. Use synthetic
inputs; a real-user study requires a separate consent and privacy plan. This
source proposal includes no owner reports, participant answers or efficacy claim.
