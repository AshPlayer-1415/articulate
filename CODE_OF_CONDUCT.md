# Articulate community guidelines

Help make this a useful place for people building and using AI tools.

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique the idea or behavior, not the person.
- Welcome different backgrounds and levels of experience. Explain assumptions.
- Stay relevant to the question, workflow or change being discussed.
- Protect privacy. Share synthetic examples, not another person's conversations,
  profiles, credentials, private code or identifying details.
- Share only content you have permission to publish. Disclose relevant affiliations
  when recommending a product or service.
- Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, spam and deliberate exposure of
  private information are not welcome.

Maintainers may edit, remove or lock harmful content and restrict participation
when needed. For a conduct concern, email [hello@i-ops.dev](mailto:hello@i-ops.dev).
Avoid publishing personal details when reporting a concern. Security vulnerabilities
have a separate [private reporting route](SECURITY.md).
