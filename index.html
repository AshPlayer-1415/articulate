THE IDEA BEHIND ARTICULATE

Same information.
A different way in.

Imagine ten people reading the same technical guide. One asks for an example. Another wants the steps. A third wants the reasoning behind the decision.

Think about the last time something finally made sense. What helped: a concrete example, a connection to work you already knew, or a chance to try it yourself?

That is the motivation behind Articulate: give you and your AI a clearer, shared starting point for how to work through things together. Your preferences can change with the task, the project and what you already know.

Today, the local pilot starts with requests you have actually made in supported coding-agent history. You review what it finds and decide what travels to another agent.

CONVERSATION MEMORY

What should carry forward?

Past messages, facts and decisions give an agent continuity.

ARTICULATE’S FOCUS

How should we work through it?

Reviewed preferences add guidance for explaining and collaborating—such as walking through an example before the abstraction, or showing the cause before a fix.

Memory tools can support personalization too. Our focus is making your reviewed working preferences portable across agents. The pilot does not measure intelligence or claim proven learning gains.

THE FLOW

Bring the context.
Keep the control.

Start with one project or your work across projects. Choose your agents when you’re ready to connect.

  1. 01 ↗

    Bring your work together.

    Find supported local activity from Codex, Claude Code and Cursor. Articulate can use the traces available on your device; cloud-only and deleted chats may be missing.

  2. 02 ↗

    Review what carries forward.

    Check suggested preferences, edit them, or keep your own. The default scan finds explicitly stated requests. Broader interpretation needs a configured local model.

  3. 03 ↗

    Connect on your terms.

    Choose which agents can receive your reviewed preferences. Each connection needs its own approval. Use profiles and saved workflows for different kinds of work.

Read the privacy notes ↗
01

History stays on your device.

Raw imported activity is not returned through Articulate’s MCP tools. Connected AI providers can process the reviewed context you choose to share.

02

Preferences, with boundaries.

They guide how an agent presents its work. Your current task, facts and safety still take priority. A stored preference is not proof of a better answer.

03

Change your mind whenever.

Review profiles, revoke an agent’s access, or erase Articulate’s local data through the CLI. Removing a package and deleting saved data are separate steps.
